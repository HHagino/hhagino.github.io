# Part I. Why this is a relative trace

## 1. Kernel diagonal rather than an absolute representation trace

Let `P_q` denote the Bergman projection on the universal-cover holomorphic `q`-differential Hilbert space and let `U_{\delta^m}` be the canonical unitary transport. Formally the weighted transported operator is

\[
M_WP_qU_{\delta^m}P_q.
\]

Its universal-cover diagonal is periodic under the primitive centralizer `\langle\delta\rangle`. The natural trace density therefore descends not to a finite trace on the full representation space, but to the centralizer quotient `Y_\delta`.

Definition (0.1) is precisely the diagonal integral over one primitive centralizer fundamental domain. In this sense it is a **relative/cyclic trace**: one divides out the infinite repetition generated by the centralizer before integrating the diagonal.

No assertion of trace-classness on the full discrete-series Hilbert space is required.

Let

## 3. Centralizer averaging is forced

Since the transported diagonal depends only on the transverse coordinate `u`,

The primitive length `ell`, rather than `m ell`, appears because the quotient is by the full centralizer of `delta^m`, namely the primitive cyclic group `\langle\delta\rangle`.
